个人简介

我是深圳理工大学算力微电子学院教研助理教授、博士生导师，曾师从香港科技大学（广州）吕杨迪教授。研究聚焦芯片验证与电子设计自动化（EDA），已在 DATE、ICCAD、ICCD、MLCAD、ACM TODAES 等会议和期刊发表或录用论文 10 余篇，并曾参与 DATE、DAC、HOST、GLSVLSI 等会议的审稿工作。

主要研究方向：

  • 处理器与 SoC 前硅验证：覆盖率驱动测试、RTL 断言生成，以及仿真与形式化协同验证。
  • EDA 工具与芯片设计自动化：仿真、逻辑综合、布局和可测试性设计（DFT），以及 AI 在 EDA 中的应用。
  • RTL 解析与形式化验证：RTL 结构化建模、中间表示（IR）分析、符号执行与神经符号方法。

我希望与学生作为科研伙伴一起提出问题、验证想法和探索解决方案。团队氛围开放自由，尊重个人节奏，提供充足的科研支持、芯片流片及产业交流机会。欢迎有好奇心和自驱力的博士生、硕士生、博士后、科研助理及实习生申请，简历请发送至 zhengziyu@suat-sz.edu.cn。

About

I am an Assistant Professor and Ph.D. Advisor in the School of Computing and Microelectronics at Shenzhen University of Technology. I previously worked under the supervision of Prof. Yangdi Lyu at HKUST(GZ). My research focuses on chip verification and electronic design automation (EDA). I have published or had accepted more than 10 papers at venues including DATE, ICCAD, ICCD, MLCAD, and ACM TODAES, and have reviewed for DATE, DAC, HOST, and GLSVLSI.

Research Interests:

  • Pre-silicon verification of processors and SoCs: Coverage-driven testing, RTL assertion generation, and combined simulation and formal verification.
  • EDA tools and chip design automation: Simulation, logic synthesis, placement, design for testability (DFT), and AI for EDA.
  • RTL parsing and formal verification: Structured RTL modeling, intermediate representation (IR) analysis, symbolic execution, and neuro-symbolic methods.

I see advising as a research partnership: asking questions, testing ideas, and exploring solutions together. Our group offers an open, flexible environment, strong research support, chip tape-out opportunities, and industry connections. We welcome curious and self-motivated Ph.D. and master's students, postdoctoral researchers, research assistants, and interns. Please send your CV to zhengziyu@suat-sz.edu.cn.
